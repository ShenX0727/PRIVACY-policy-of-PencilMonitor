功能

Features

一支笔能说的，它都读得出来

Everything the pencil reports, read out loud

四个标签页覆盖从概览到实测的完整流程：先看状态，再做体检，然后用画板与实时数据自己验证。

Four tabs cover the whole flow: check the status, run the tests, then verify it yourself on the canvas and in the live readouts.

概览

Dashboard

设备与笔的连接状态、能力清单、四项实时读数（压力 / 倾斜 / 旋转 / 悬停距离），以及一个跟着笔动的 3D 姿态视图。

Connection status for the iPad and the pencil, a capability list, four live readouts (pressure, tilt, roll, hover distance) and a 3D pose view that follows the pencil.

硬件体检

Hardware tests

悬停截止距离与光标偏移、压力 / 倾角 / 旋转三通道的量程覆盖评级、笔身触觉反馈，以及挤压与双击的阶段与时长。

Hover cutoff distance and cursor offset, range-coverage grades for the pressure, tilt and roll channels, barrel haptics, plus the phases and duration of squeeze and double-tap.

画板

Canvas

PencilKit 画布与官方工具盘，四种纸张纹理。悬停时在笔尖下渲染整支笔的剪影投影，近实远虚。支持撤销、笔迹统计与导出。

A PencilKit canvas with Apple's own tool picker and four paper textures. On hover it casts a silhouette of the whole pencil under the tip — sharp up close, soft further away. Undo, stroke stats and export included.

实时数据

Live data

示波式读数面板：屏幕坐标、按压力度（0–255）、倾角与方位角（弧度）、侧旋指针，逐帧跟随。

An oscilloscope-style panel: screen coordinates, force (0–255), altitude and azimuth in radians, and a barrel-roll dial — updated every frame.

设备健康报告

Health report

把设备信息与体检结果汇总成一份报告，可导出文本或 PDF，用系统分享发送给需要的人。

Device details and test results collected into one report, exportable as text or PDF and sent through the system share sheet.

外观与语言

Look and language

八款 App 图标、两套主题配色、深浅跟随或手动指定，中英双语切换即时生效。

Eight app icons, two colour themes, light and dark on system or by hand, and an instant switch between 中文 and English.

隐私

Privacy

数据一条都不上传

Nothing ever leaves the device

应用不含网络传输功能，不设账号，未接入统计分析、崩溃上报或广告组件，也不跨应用追踪。 所有读数都在本机处理与显示。

There is no network code, no account system, no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads and no cross-app tracking. Every reading is processed and shown on the device itself.
